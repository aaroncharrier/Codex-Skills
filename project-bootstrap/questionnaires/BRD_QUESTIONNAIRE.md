# BRD Completion Questionnaire

Use this questionnaire to gather the inputs needed to draft a Business Requirements Document for a data analytics project. Answer briefly and concretely. If an answer is unknown, write `TBD` and name the owner who will resolve it.

## 1. Executive Summary
1. What is the initiative called in business terms?
2. In one or two sentences, what change is the business requesting?
3. What business value should this initiative create?
4. Why is this work needed now?

## 2. Business Problem / Opportunity
1. What business problem exists today?
2. Who is affected by the problem?
3. What decisions, workflows, or outcomes are being harmed?
4. How is the problem handled today?
5. What evidence shows the problem is real: delays, rework, low trust, missed SLA, manual effort?

## 3. Business Goals and Objectives
1. What are the top three business outcomes this project must deliver?
2. Which goal matters most if tradeoffs are required?
3. What measurable target is expected for each goal?
4. What deadline or business event drives those goals?

## 4. Stakeholders and Roles
1. Who is the executive sponsor?
2. Who owns the business process affected by this project?
3. Who owns the source data or certified metric definition?
4. Which teams will consume the output: operations, finance, merchandising, BI, leadership?
5. Who must approve scope, requirements, and go-live?

## 5. Current State
1. What data, reports, or processes are used today?
2. Where are manual steps or workarounds happening?
3. What trust, freshness, or completeness issues exist today?
4. Which systems are missing data or showing conflicting values?
5. How long does the current process take?

## 6. Future State
1. What should be true for the business after the project is complete?
2. What decisions should users be able to make more effectively?
3. What manual work should be reduced or eliminated?
4. What should business users trust or see that they cannot today?

## 7. Business Scope
1. Which business domains are in scope: inventory, sales, customer, supplier, finance?
2. Which business units, regions, or user groups are included?
3. Which dashboards, datasets, or metric families are included?
4. Is this phase 1 only, or the full business rollout?

## 8. Out of Scope
1. What related requests are explicitly not part of this effort?
2. Are historical backfills excluded? If yes, to what extent?
3. Are upstream process changes excluded?
4. Are report redesigns or new BI platforms excluded?

## 9. Assumptions and Constraints
1. What are you assuming about source availability, stakeholder access, or data ownership?
2. What business deadlines or blackout periods constrain delivery?
3. Are there governance, compliance, or approval constraints?
4. Are there limits on budget, staffing, or delivery sequence?

## 10. Risks and Dependencies
1. What could prevent the business outcome from being achieved?
2. Which upstream teams or systems does this work depend on?
3. Which approvals or policy reviews are required?
4. What happens to the business if the project is delayed or the data is late?

## 11. Business Requirements
1. What must the business be able to do once this work is complete?
2. What freshness expectation exists for the data or report?
3. What trust or validation expectation must be met before users rely on the output?
4. What exception visibility is required when data is missing, late, or invalid?
5. What business rules or definitions must be standardized?

## 12. Success Criteria
1. How will the business know the project succeeded?
2. What measurable indicators should improve after launch?
3. What adoption or usage result is expected?
4. What minimum SLA or service level must be achieved?

## 13. Approvals
1. Who signs off on the business problem statement?
2. Who signs off on scope and out-of-scope decisions?
3. Who signs off on metric definitions or business rules?
4. Who gives final approval to move into build or release?

## Final Check
1. Are any answers still `TBD`?
2. Which unanswered items block BRD completion?
3. Who owns each unresolved item, and by what date?
