# Question Bank - MicroStrategy Reporting Development

Ask exactly 3 questions at a time.

## User and Outcome Discovery
- Who are the primary users?
- What decisions should the report or object support?
- What is frustrating or missing today?
- What should users be able to see or do after release?

## Reporting Discovery
- Which dashboards, dossiers, reports, or objects are in scope?
- Which KPIs or metrics must appear?
- What exact business definitions are required?
- What filters, drill paths, or breakdowns are required?
- What freshness expectation do users have?
- What should users see when data is stale or incomplete?

## Acceptance and Release
- What would make the output trustworthy?
- What would block user acceptance?
- Who approves the output?
