# Question Bank - Snowflake Semantic Layer / Analytics Modeling

Ask exactly 3 questions at a time.

## Business and Consumption Discovery
- What analytics use case does this model or view support?
- Who consumes it?
- What business decisions depend on it?
- What is wrong with the current structure?

## Modeling Discovery
- What is the target grain?
- What are the business keys?
- Which joins or dimensions are required?
- Are conformed dimensions required?
- Are SCD or historical views needed?
- Which fields or metrics require exact business definitions?

## Quality and Delivery
- What validation is required before release?
- What performance expectations exist?
- What downstream tools depend on this remaining stable?
