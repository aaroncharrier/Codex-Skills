# Question Bank - Existing Pipeline Enhancement / Optimization

Ask exactly 3 questions at a time.

## Current-State Discovery
- What existing pipeline is being changed?
- What is the pain: failures, performance, cost, SLA, quality, maintainability?
- What evidence exists for the problem?

## Technical Discovery
- What part of the pipeline is changing?
- What downstream contracts could break?
- Is the problem volume, logic, orchestration, infrastructure, or support?
- Are schema or business-rule changes involved?
- Is backfill or reprocessing needed?

## Quality and Operations
- What monitoring gaps exist?
- What DQ gaps exist?
- What support burden should be reduced?
- What should be measurably better after the change?
